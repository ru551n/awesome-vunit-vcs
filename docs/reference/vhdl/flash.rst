Flash
=====

The packages, contexts and entities of the flash family: the QSPI NOR flash, the QSPI master and the
QSPI protocol checker. Testbenches use ``flash_context``; ``qspi_pkg`` holds the bus types both ends of
the bus share. :doc:`../../flash/index` explains how the pieces fit together.
